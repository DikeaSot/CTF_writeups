# Title: Symbols

**Category**: Crypto

**Description**: Some ciphers are really just weird fonts.

ΧΤΦκομ{εϖ3ρ_η34ρδ_αβ0υ7_τη1σ_φ0ντ?}

Flag format: CTFkom{...}

----

We are provided with a ciphertext.

At first glance, it looks like Greek to me (literally) so apparently the key to this challenge is just being Greek. Then the challenge becomes so easy.

By replacing the "weird font" with the equivalent latin charachters, we get the plaintext:
```CTFkom{ev3r_h34rd_ab0u7_th1s_f0nt?}```
